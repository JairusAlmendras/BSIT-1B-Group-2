Cleanliness & Recycling Guide

This is a simple guide about waste categories and practical ways to reduce waste at home and in the community.

Waste segregation

Biodegradable Waste

Examples include fruit peels, vegetable scraps, and leaves. Follow local guidance for composting or collection.

Waste segregation

Non-biodegradable Waste

Some materials take a long time to break down. Reduce their use and dispose of them through the proper local system.

Recycling

Paper and Cardboard

Keep recyclable paper clean and dry. Check whether your local collection accepts each type.

Recycling

Plastic and Metal Containers

Empty containers and follow local instructions about rinsing, sorting, and accepted materials.

Cleanliness

Keep Public Spaces Clean

Use proper bins, avoid littering, and report overflowing public waste bins to the appropriate local office.

Cleanliness

Join Community Clean-ups

Participate in organized clean-up activities and follow the safety instructions of event organizers.

Reminder: Waste categories and collection rules may differ by locality. Follow your local government's waste-management instructions.
