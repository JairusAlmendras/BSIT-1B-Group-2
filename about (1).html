About the project

About Our Community Guide

A simple educational website that encourages responsible waste management and community cleanliness.

Why this website was created

------This website is designed to educate users and visitors by providing basic knowledge, helpful tips, and practical ideas on how to keep their communities and neighborhoods clean.

------It also aims to make information more accessible by publishing the website online, allowing people to easily visit and learn from it anytime.

Our Mission

To encourage responsible waste management and practical community action through clear, accessible information.

Our Vision

Cleaner, healthier communities where people work together to reduce waste and protect shared spaces.

Our Core Values

These values guide the message of the website.

🍀

Responsibility

Take responsibility for the waste we create and the places we share.

🤝

Cooperation

Work together with neighbors and community groups.

🌏

Sustainability

Choose habits that help protect resources for the future.
